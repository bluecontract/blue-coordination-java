# 3.0.0-rc.1 Round 11 readiness

This candidate retains the Round 10.1 Process Embedded architecture and adds a
focused Round 11 closure: a committed document cannot become application-
readable until its business epoch, published graph epoch, ready epoch, direct
managed-child bindings, cursors, barriers, and pending admissions agree.

## Readiness boundary

Frozen PROCESS commits the exact document revision first and leaves the session
`CATCHING_UP`. Coordination publishes routes and the Process Embedded graph,
settles child work and parent cursors, and only then publishes `READY` for that
same epoch. `document()` fails closed on incomplete evidence;
`auditDocument()` remains the explicit committed-state diagnostic view.

Historical completeness is keyed to the immutable active source surface rather
than unrelated mutable business BlueId, allowing reuse after business-only
changes and invalidation after real route/subscription changes.

## Capability classification

The generic Timeline Entry model's missing universal literal `documentId` is an
optional generalized targeting-profile capability, not an engine blocker. The
environment derives recipients, a single entry may target several documents,
and concrete Channel/message types can define exact target derivation.
Repository-native `OperationRequest.document` targeting remains supported.

Exact provider-backed Mandate eligibility at source order remains a real
agentic blocker. It is not inferred, emulated, or bypassed.

## Publication boundary

Published dependency preflight is `BLOCKED_UPSTREAM`: Repository rc.19 and
Language rc.20 resolve, but BEX core/contracts rc.3 are missing and
commons-codec 1.15 conflicts with 1.11. The published external-consumer smoke
build therefore cannot proceed. The exact candidate commit and extracted source
archive are the remaining evidence-finalization items; local-composite success
cannot substitute for published-artifact resolution.

## Deliberate scope

The candidate remains a sequential, single-process, in-memory library. It does
not claim provider-backed fresh-process durability, distributed transactions,
cross-process exactly-once behavior, Mandate-backed authority, sub-second
frozen Wadowice processing, or canonical Repository-domain Wadowice
compatibility.
