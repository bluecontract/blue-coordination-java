# POC architecture

> **Design baseline:** 15.12 · **Status:** synchronized with Phase1/2 handoff; general integration pending

[Start with examples](tutorial/README.md) · [Independent-lineage boundary](18-independent-lineage-processing.md) · [Causal order](17-causal-processing-model.md)

The owning libraries and PostgreSQL foundation are implemented and verified in the scope recorded
in [24](24-phase-1-library-summary.md) and the [readiness report](implementation/phase-1-2-readiness.md).
The end-to-end loop below remains the Phase3 integration target, not the default MyOS application

## Responsibilities

| Component | Owns | Does not own |
|---|---|---|
| Contracts | Exact processing within one invocation: work/event order, gas, tentative state, rollback and final identities. | Database transactions, leases or cross-invocation scheduling. |
| Coordination | Canonical local cause/receipt selection, temporal recipient and dependency rules, occurrence progress and validated incremental effects needed to maintain host indexes/work. | SQL, queue architecture, wall-clock ordering or merging all observers by connectivity. |
| MyOS | Durable execution of every due reaction or its specified terminal failure; transactionally coherent temporal dependency indexes, ready-work selection, resource acquisition, atomic commit, recovery and ordered publication. | Inventing Blue routing, operation/gas boundaries or history semantics. |

MyOS Mini is the POC host. The proposal does not integrate the new document runtime into production
myos-java; it reuses the latter's Timeline provider foundation.

## The processing loop

1. MyOS durably records exact Timeline input, or a Timeline-free admission request and its reservation.
2. Coordination selects the eligible external cause using complete normalized Timeline evidence.
3. Coordination derives the selected lineage's local operation and exact dependency/read scope.
4. It derives eligible work from local predecessors, occurrence cursors and complete causal frontiers.
5. MyOS supplies the requested current scoped facts and immutable exact material.
6. Coordination invokes the owning interpreter for the selected work. A core result may contain
   several dependency-ordered, independently atomic owned-group operations; each contains its
   complete semantic outcome. Missing evidence does not authorize partial publication of an operation.
7. MyOS commits each complete independent operation with its settlement, cursor/obligations and
   applicable outbox projections. It retains durable continuation between those commits; it never
   treats one `evaluate` return as a mandatory graph-wide transaction or splits one owned group.
8. Source publication makes its retained result and required semantic observation evidence durably
   discoverable to observing occurrences. A final-state receipt alone may be insufficient.
9. Each eligible Order consumes the exact due receipt and commits its own reaction. Healthy siblings
   and the one-way source continue when another Order waits or fails.
10. Optionally report aggregate completion from complete delivery/lifecycle coverage and outcomes.
    This reporting step creates no Contracts invocation, epoch or semantic gas and gates no independent commit.

The detailed action laws are in [17](17-causal-processing-model.md). This list is not a replacement
phase comparator. [21](21-semantic-equivalence-and-source-reuse.md) requires an independently
specified observation trace before fixing grouping/pin/update/admission phases. In particular,
FULL_HISTORY must not import an initially embedded source's whole future history before an
earlier parent observation; physical factorization must not remove ordinary ancestor delivery.

## Three different frozen facts

**External intent:** the selected cause/cutoff, admission policy and exact selected local deliveries.
Unrelated future input cannot enter that operation. Global fanout discovery need not finish before
source publication; recipient eligibility uses authoritative temporal lifecycle indexes and their
processing/coverage frontiers, not an unqualified snapshot of only today's links.

**Historical basis:** the exact history required by the attachment policy and accepted boundary.
Receipts generated by the active causal computation are separately authenticated obligations.
They are not confused with unrelated future input.

The original source-event order is provenance, not the monotonic position of a later consumer
revision. Two distinct imports may carry the same origin; an import after attachment K100 may
carry origin E10. Its producer/receipt chain determines visibility: E10 alone cannot make the
K100 effect visible under an earlier cutoff of 50. Its newly generated obligations belong to K100,
not to the already closed E10 completion scope.

**Invocation input:** the exact scoped pre-state and delivery evidence for one Contracts call.
It is frozen for that invocation. The next independently committed invocation can use a newer scoped
state after proving its own exact predecessor and dependencies. Unrelated sibling commits are not
identity-bearing input and cannot invalidate local semantic intent.

When a local result already selects a concrete successor, it needs that selection authority but not
bytes used only to execute the successor. Commit its exact progress/references and fetch that body
later. Source publication is different: a valid durable discovery basis suffices before particular
consumer successors are selected. It must not wait for all recipient enumeration or parent payloads.

A plan does not pin one global graph cut across every Order. Durable discovery retains source
history until every eligible occurrence is accounted for. Dependency indexes are authoritative,
transactionally consistent with committed lifecycle history, not best-effort caches. A target whose
earlier topology operation is still unprocessed has an explicit incomplete frontier; the index does
not pretend that operation never exists. Normal discovery uses indexed temporal queries and durable
registration/progress changes, not repeated history reconstruction or scans of all observers' bodies.

## Catch-up and source feedback

A parent can contain an older exact source value while the source's authoritative current head is newer.
Import advances the particular occurrence's historical view; it does not downgrade the source.

A reaction during import may create a real returning dependency. If it is workflow feedback within
one logical operation, it must execute under that operation's atomic scope/shared gas; do not turn
each hop into a later independently budgeted receipt. Genuinely separate operations need exact
producer/receiver dependencies and canonical positions, never worker-arrival order. Independent
one-way observers do not impose that barrier. Prove the boundary rather than infer it from an SCC.

Readiness is scoped to the selected operation: an unrelated live operation must not observe an
unfinished dependency; a historical replay operation may observe the exact historical state its
chronological cursor authorizes. Authenticate the exact producer, receipt, dependency and temporal
eligibility; they may span different causes. Sharing a root is neither necessary nor sufficient
permission to read partially processed state.

Historical replacement/removal of an embedding is separately planned: A@T20 may leave B after B@T10
without first importing B@T30. Retire only that occurrence's obsolete obligations and derive any new
occurrence under its declared policy. The baseline Contracts pending-binding restriction required a
reconciliation change, now included in Phase1. A working historical read alone does not establish
the full durable integration capability.

## Initialization preparation

Separate authorized admission from preparation/materialization. Rebuilding an already authorized
source is not rebirth, and cache presence cannot choose history or gas. A new authored child,
an initialized epoch-0 input and a retained historical source are not interchangeable inputs.

A prepared initialization candidate follows22's canonical source origin and fixed source gas policy,
not the baseline creator-owned initialization scope. Successful authorization promotes it idempotently;
failed creators publish no new source authority/events/charges. Already independently admitted sources
survive parent failure. FROM_* policies select observer history while source history remains canonical
FULL_HISTORY. This explicitly changes owning-library semantics. See [22](22-processing-kernel.md).

## Atomicity and recovery

Agreement's local source operation and each ordinary Order reaction commit independently in both live
and historical processing. Each complete local result remains atomic. Phase1 implements this owning-
library change to the baseline active-parent closure boundary; the host does not split a result.
One Order's deterministic failure rolls back only its defined local operation and blocks actual
dependents; it cannot undo Agreement or healthy siblings. Earlier complete steps survive an outage.

Current Contracts returns Complete or NeedsResources, not a serialized continuation. Retry starts
again; identity-bearing occurrence evidence may rebuild the invocation under the same stable
application. Unknown commit is reconciled from durable settlement before execution is repeated.

MyOS uses scoped target/occurrence/lifecycle fences for coherent mutable reads and short commit-time
locking. A global physical revision is not a semantic predecessor or independent-sibling retry cause.
It never holds a lock while waiting for content. The trusted adapter maintains
indexes in the same transaction as their source state; tests audit query completeness independently.

## Publication

Every committed business terminal action appends one immutable batch, including an empty batch, to its
lineage/stream's ordered predecessor chain. The publisher verifies causal prerequisites and the exact next batch or a bounded page.
Ongoing appends cannot invalidate an already verified immutable successor.

Required observation evidence must retain original events separately from new emissions, exact
views/updates at observable boundaries and event-admission/FIFO order. Its derivation and validation
are owning-library work, not an opaque host hash. Do not require every ancestor delivery to pass
through a committed Parent relay; prove the logical route and avoid direct-plus-relay duplicates.
Verified same-epoch representation companions belong to their triggering result/delta: required
representation publication is atomic but creates no new business receipt, event/fanout or gas reset.

Publication is visible per completed semantic action. Whole-cause completion, if reported, is a separate fact.
ACK loss can repeat transport, but the sink durably deduplicates deterministic event keys.

Operation-kind-specific progress, deterministic initialization/gas, complete per-reaction-origin consumer
groups and visibility obligations are specified in [19](19-semantic-edge-contracts.md) and
[21](21-semantic-equivalence-and-source-reuse.md).
The scoped history/live registration protocol and separate processing/discovery frontiers are in
[18](18-independent-lineage-processing.md#4-delivery-completeness-and-lifecycle). Storage primitives
and bridge tests are present; the general mapping/pumps still need Phase3 verification. An opaque
evidence value or database row does not itself supply the semantic algorithm.

## Host work selection and scale

Efficient work selection is an architectural requirement, although the concrete queues, indexes,
partitioning and batching belong to MyOS. The target is millions of stored documents, potentially
thousands active for one user, and one Agreement with 1,000/10,000/100,000 independent Orders.

MyOS atomically updates document/occurrence state, temporal dependency indexes and the durable basis
for resulting work. Indexed source-to-consumer lookup discovers due reactions in bounded pages;
reverse dependency/waiter lookup wakes only affected work; indexed ready-work selection does not
poll every document or every document of a user. A source commit persists resumable fanout authority,
not N pre-created jobs. Coordination supplies the semantic predicates and validated incremental
effects; MyOS must realize complete scoped queries and crash-safe scheduling from them.

The PostgreSQL POC must specify actual access paths and inspect their plans under high inactive and
active cardinalities. See [storage access patterns](06-storage-and-transactions.md#1002-indexed-work-selection-is-a-host-contract).
No global or per-user document scan, full-history rebuild, or N-recipient transaction belongs to the
ordinary scheduling path. Optional aggregate reporting is not a prerequisite for useful local work.

## POC limits and performance

One-way source/consumer operations can advance independently inside an isolated host domain.
Same-target conflicts and returning dependencies require canonical serialization/completeness,
not arbitrary worker ordering. No cross-domain graph edges, distributed transaction, relocation,
version profiles or migration are introduced.
Dynamic channels inside the configured Timeline superset are supported; an outside-set subscription
is an explicit nonterminal supported-scope hold.

The fast path keeps a bounded working set hot across required commits. It does not add a transaction
per edge or event. Test one Agreement with 1/10/100/1000/10000/100000 Orders, and a large dormant graph
with a small required spine. Agreement commit latency depends on its own required work, not completion
of N observers. Total fanout time may grow with N: required consumer reactions are real, metered work,
not a failure to meet one universal seconds-scale deadline. Bounded memory, transactions, concurrency,
backpressure, fair service and tenant isolation must preserve stability under sustained backlog.
Vary P placements in one consumer separately from N consumers; group scope/gas cannot
be split when a host cap is too small, and a hold must name a real capacity releaser. Measure
receipt decoding, verification, hashing and copies separately from source business calls to expose
avoidable amplification, not to demand constant-cost N-consumer processing. Measure
graph metadata separately from exact-content bytes; no unexplained
whole-component scan is hidden behind a locality claim.
